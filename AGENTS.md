# History Book Agent Operating Contract

This is the single repository-wide operating contract for the History Book project.

The project-specific method owner is [docs/BOOK_BUILD_SYSTEM.md](docs/BOOK_BUILD_SYSTEM.md). Use [templates/SECTION_BUILD_CONTRACT.md](templates/SECTION_BUILD_CONTRACT.md) as the compact execution packet for individual sections. External MAPS_L, Rung, THINK, PLAN, philosophy, and Design Bible files are methods/references, not competing project authority.

## Authority and precedence

Use this order:

1. Explicit human instruction.
2. This `AGENTS.md`.
3. Approved History Book project/roadmap scope when added.
4. The active section/task contract.
5. [docs/BOOK_BUILD_SYSTEM.md](docs/BOOK_BUILD_SYSTEM.md).
6. Evidence/research/source records.
7. Templates/examples.
8. External method references.

Capability is not permission. Do not silently expand the book's approved scope or publish externally without authority.

## Core rules

1. **Evidence outranks narrative.** Change the prose when evidence contradicts it.
2. **Do not guess across material uncertainty.** Use `VERIFIED`, `REPORTED`, `INTERPRETATION`, `ASSUMED`, `DISPUTED`, and `UNKNOWN` when the distinction matters.
3. **No predetermined national verdict.** Do not force evidence into either patriotic mythology or reflexive anti-American counter-mythology.
4. **Primary evidence first where practical.** Prefer recoverable primary sources, then high-quality institutional collections and specialist scholarship.
5. **Primary sources are evidence, not automatic truth.** A speech proves what was said; it does not automatically prove the speaker's factual claims or private motives.
6. **Preserve serious disagreement.** Do not average incompatible interpretations or manufacture false balance.
7. **Context explains; it does not automatically excuse.** Reconstruct what historical actors knew, believed, feared, wanted, and could realistically do.
8. **Separate chronology from causation.** Major causal claims need a plausible mechanism and evidence, not merely sequence.
9. **Modern connections require a bridge.** Show the chain from historical event → later development → surviving modern structure before claiming causation.
10. **Keep the simple story simple until it fails.** Give novices a usable model first, then add complexity where evidence requires it.
11. **Teach, do not merely tell.** Major conceptual sections should help readers reason about a fresh case, following the Rung transfer principle.
12. **Use proportional process.** Routine factual passages do not need the same machinery as disputed, load-bearing interpretations.
13. **One concept, one owner.** Link to canonical project documents rather than copying mutable guidance into multiple files.
14. **Independent review for consequential synthesis.** A writer should not be the sole approver of a major disputed or load-bearing interpretation.
15. **Do not stop at a child task while authorized parent work remains.** Reconcile the result and continue until the parent outcome is complete or genuinely blocked.

## Standard section route

```text
recover parent/chapter context
→ define the section's central historical question
→ inspect current evidence and unknowns
→ THINK when framing/alternatives matter
→ PLAN the smallest research capable of changing the conclusion
→ research claims against recoverable sources
→ synthesize
→ draft using the standard narrative structure
→ Rung teaching/transfer pass
→ information-design pass where useful
→ verify and independently review at proportional risk
→ correct/research/reframe as needed
→ record completion and continue
```

## Required source handling

For important claims, preserve enough information for another researcher to recover the source and verify the use:

- source title/author or institution;
- exact URL/path/edition where relevant;
- page, section, date, document identifier, or other precise locator when available;
- what claim the source supports;
- source type and limitations;
- uncertainty or disagreement that materially affects the conclusion.

Do not retain a load-bearing claim whose evidence cannot be recovered.

## Review outcomes

Use:

- `APPROVED` — acceptance criteria are met.
- `CHANGES_REQUESTED` — a concrete correctness/evidence/teaching failure remains.
- `BLOCKED — NEEDS RESEARCH` — available evidence is insufficient for the required conclusion.

Do not block merely because a reviewer would prefer different prose when the agreed criteria are satisfied.

## Recovery

```text
local evidence problem      → RESEARCH / CORRECT
research-structure problem  → PLAN
broken explanatory frame    → THINK
credible unresolved dispute → preserve DISPUTED / UNKNOWN
section no longer useful    → CUT / MERGE / RESTRUCTURE
true authority crossing     → HUMAN REAUTHORIZATION
```
