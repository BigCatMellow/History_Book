## Author-side adversarial questions

### Could the section be read as “old Republicans were basically modern Democrats”?

No. The draft explicitly rejects one-to-one mapping and notes that older Whig/Jacksonian traditions contain elements with analogues in different modern coalitions.

### Could it be read as minimizing Republican abolition or civil-rights history?

The draft explicitly states Republican leadership in abolition and Reconstruction and Republican necessity in the 1964 cloture coalition.

### Could it be read as saying race/civil rights was irrelevant to realignment?

No. It identifies race/civil rights as central while rejecting a monocausal explanation for a century-scale coalition change.

### Could it be read as saying every Southern Democrat personally became a Republican?

No. It explains voter movement, generational replacement, mixed ballots, and slow down-ballot change.

### Could it be read as saying the parties never changed until 1964?

No. It identifies the New Deal Black-voter shift and earlier cross-party ideological coalitions.

## Material limitation before publication-ready status

The current evidence spine is strong for a **worked overview**, but it leans heavily on institutional historical syntheses from the Senate, House, Library of Congress, Miller Center, and Pew.

A publication-ready chapter should add:

1. selected primary-source excerpts/records at key transition points;
2. specialist historiography representing serious disagreements over the timing and causes of Southern realignment;
3. deeper evidence on state/local vs presidential realignment timing;
4. a source-quality pass on any modern analogy retained in final prose.

This does not block the worked example from demonstrating the build system. It **does** block treating the section as final publication copy.

## Process findings from the test run

### What worked

- THINK prevented the false binary of “party switch” vs “nothing changed.”
- PLAN focused research on claims that discriminate among those frames.
- The claim-evidence matrix made it difficult to slide from party-label continuity to ideological continuity.
- Rung produced a clear teaching target: distinguish organization, coalition, faction, and ideology.
- The review gate prevented author confidence from becoming `DONE`.

### Friction / possible system refinement

For a high-risk topic, the full build record is justified. For ordinary sections it would be too much.

The compact template should remain the default, with deeper THINK/PLAN/evidence expansion triggered by:
- disputed causation;
- politically consequential interpretation;
- material modern connection;
- high downstream dependence;
- meaningful source disagreement.
